Good questions. Little answers.

How do I solve a rectangle?

A rectangle must contain exactly one number and exactly that many cells. Tap two opposite corners. A 1 takes just one tap. Squares and straight rows or columns count as rectangles; L-shapes do not. The number can be anywhere inside the rectangle.

What happens when I use a hint?

After you earn the reward and close the optional hint ad, one unsolved rectangle is completed for you. A glow highlights where the reward went. Hints do not deduct points.

I ran out of carrots. What can I do?

Each round starts with three carrot lives. An incorrect rectangle costs one carrot. You can watch a rewarded ad for three new carrots and keep your board, or restart the level. After the ad, your last incorrect selection is briefly highlighted in red.

An ad or its reward did not appear.

Ads need an internet connection and may not always be available. Finish the rewarded ad and close it to return to the game. If a reward is missing, tell us whether it was a hint or extra lives, the level and your device/iOS version. You can try again later if an ad cannot load.

When does the Daily Challenge reset?

A new daily challenge starts at midnight UTC. Your Daily calendar marks completed days and lets you browse previous months. The calendar shows your history; it does not unlock missed puzzles. Daily awards double points, with weekly points awarded once per day.

Are the players in Gigi’s Club real?

Gigi’s Club uses computer-controlled club friends on a fixed weekly schedule. The separate Game Center tab shows real-player rankings. Club scores are not sent to Game Center.

How do Game Center rankings and medals work?

Connect using the Game Center tab. The weekly leaderboard resets on Monday at 00:00 UTC. Podium achievements depend on your final place in a completed Game Center week, not a temporary live ranking. Achievement availability also depends on the Game Center configuration and connection.

Where is my progress saved?

Puzzle progress, Daily history, settings and local records are saved on your device. They may be included in an iOS device backup, depending on your settings. Bunbrix does not currently provide its own cloud progress sync. Removing the app or changing devices may affect local progress; Game Center scores do not restore your puzzle saves.

Can I replay a level or undo a rectangle?

Yes. Open a completed level in Levels to play it again. Undo removes the latest solved rectangle in the current unfinished round and adjusts its points. Replays do not add extra campaign or weekly points.

How do I change the language, sound or vibrations?

Open the gear icon, then choose Language or Sound & haptics. Bunbrix supports English, Swedish, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Portuguese, Arabic, Thai and Korean.

Your privacy matters.

Bunbrix uses Google AdMob for ads and offers optional Game Center features. Learn what is stored and which services are involved in our Privacy Policy.
