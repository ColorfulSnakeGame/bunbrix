PRIVACY POLICY

Your little
space to play.

Last updated: 7 November 2026

Bunbrix is developed by Kim Larsson. This policy explains the information involved when you use the game, its advertising and optional Game Center features, this website, or contact support.

Game data stored on your device

Bunbrix stores puzzle progress, scores, Daily Challenge completion dates, local club information, personal records, language preferences and sound/haptic settings locally on your device. These saves may be included in device backups depending on your iOS settings. The developer does not operate a server that receives your puzzle saves.

Deleting Bunbrix can remove its local data. This is separate from information held by third-party services or in device backups.

Google AdMob and advertising

Bunbrix uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (Google AdMob) to display banner ads, interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads for hints and extra lives.

Google’s SDK may process IP addresses, device identifiers, advertising interactions, crash diagnostics and performance information for ad delivery, measurement, security and related purposes. IP addresses may be used to estimate general location. The game does not request precise location access for these features.

Data processing depends on the SDK, ad configuration, device settings and applicable consent choices. Non-personalized ads can still involve data processing. The developer does not send your puzzle answers or local save files to Google as part of ad requests.

Learn more in Google’s Privacy Policy, Google’s advertising information, and the Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure. Device tracking permissions and advertising settings can be managed in iOS Settings. These settings may not disable all ad-related processing.

Optional Apple Game Center features

If you connect to Game Center, Bunbrix uses your Game Center player identifier to associate score submissions and achievement reporting with your account. The Game Center weekly leaderboard displays player names, scores and ranks. The app may read your completed weekly result to report a podium achievement.

Apple processes Game Center information under its own terms and privacy policy. Read Apple’s Game Center privacy information. You can manage Game Center in your device settings.

Gigi’s Club

Gigi’s Club is a local league with computer-controlled opponents. Their names and scores are fictional gameplay data, not real player accounts. Club information is stored locally and bot scores are not submitted to Game Center.

Support messages

If you email k__larsson@hotmail.com, the developer receives the email address, message and attachments you choose to send. This information is used to respond to your request and investigate the issue. Avoid sending passwords, payment details or other unnecessary sensitive information.

Support messages are kept as needed to handle the request and any related follow-up or legal requirements. You may contact the same address to ask about access, correction or deletion of support correspondence, subject to applicable requirements.

This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical request data, such as your IP address, to operate and secure its service. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement. We do not add analytics trackers, advertising scripts or contact-form tracking to this website. Email links open your own email application.

Your choices and third-party data

You can change in-game preferences in Settings, manage platform and tracking permissions in iOS, choose whether to connect to Game Center, and choose whether to watch rewarded ads. Requests about data held directly by Google or Apple should also be directed to those providers using their privacy resources. Data retention and international processing by those services are governed by their respective policies.

Children’s privacy

Bunbrix does not require a developer-managed account or ask players to submit personal details to play. Advertising and platform services may still process information as described above. If a child has sent personal information directly to support, a parent or guardian may contact us to request its removal.

Changes and contact

This policy may be updated when the game or its services change. The current version and its update date will be published here.

For privacy questions, contact Kim Larsson at k__larsson@hotmail.com.
