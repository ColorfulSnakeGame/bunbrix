SIMPLE RULES. CLEVER LITTLE MOMENTS.

Find a place for
every number.

Fill the board with rectangles. Each one contains exactly one number and that many cells. No gaps, no overlaps, no L-shapes.

A number can sit anywhere inside its rectangle — even right in the middle.

  1. 01

    A 1? Just one tap.

    Tap a 1 to complete its single cell. Gigi shows you the basics in the opening levels.

  2. 02

    Bigger rectangle? Two corners.

    Tap one corner, then its opposite. The right rectangle fills with colour.

  3. 03

    One board. One lovely solution.

    Keep going until every cell is filled. Then enjoy your victory and try the next level.
